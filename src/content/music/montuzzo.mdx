I composed this piece as a homage to Gustav Holst's "Venus", from which much of the musical inspiration comes. The music was written separately from the film, and it is intended that it should also be able to stand on its own.

The composition is part of my year-assignment, a project I have been working on over the past year, alongside the short film Montuzzo.

## About the Composition

The music captures the magic of the abandoned ruined city of Montuzzo, but especially also the power of nature when it breaks down the once man-made city.
