# Montuzzo

A film montage with a focus on the cinematography and the music.

I composed the music myself and is a homage to Gustav Holst "Venus", where much of the musical inspiration comes from. The music was written separately from the film, and it is intended that it should also be able to stand on its own.

I have tried both to capture the magic of the abandoned ruined city of Montuzzo, but especially also the power of nature when it breaks down the once man-made city.

Montuzzo is a small abandoned ruined city that you can walk to from the town of Cicogna. It is located in northern Italy in the Val Grande National Park.

I want to give a big thank you to all the inhabitants of Cicogna and to my good friend Snorre who gave me company when we spent the night in Montuzzo.

The film montage and the music are part of my year-assignment, a project I have been working on over the past year. Where I have also built my own anamorphic lens, which is the one I have also used to film with.

## Technical Details

- **Camera**: Fujifilm X-T30
- **Lens**: DIY anamorphic lens, with Helios 44m 58mm, Schneider ES Cinelux Anamorphic 2X MC and a 3d printed Vari-diopter Pfocus B
- **Music**: Original composition by Karl August Krogh Nybo
- **Locations**: Montuzzo, Cicogna, Val Grande National Park
